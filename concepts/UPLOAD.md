# Item Shop: upload sheet

Post on a **new channel**, not buh connects: different audience, different hook.
One reel per day, at the same time each day, so the three formats can be compared fairly.

## Settings (same for all three)
- Upload the MP4 from `concepts/`. It's vertical and under 3 minutes, so YouTube makes it a Short automatically.
- Audience: **No, it's not made for kids**
- Altered or synthetic content: **No** (stylized pixel animation, nothing realistic)
- Category: Education
- Comments: on
- Thumbnail (mobile app, "Edit cover"): pick the **very first frame**. It shows the question and both sides of the matchup.
- After it's live, post and **pin** the comment listed under each reel.

**Description**

## After 48-72 hours, compare in YouTube Studio
Open each Short's analytics and note:
- **Viewed vs. swiped away**: the share of people who stopped scrolling. This is the closest thing to an official Shorts score.
- **Average percentage viewed**: over 100% means people are re-watching the loop.
- **Views** and **comments**, especially comments asking for a next customer.

Whichever format wins on swipe-away rate is the one to make more of.

If one of these gets a Content ID claim on the music, dispute it: the composition is public domain and the recording is our own.
